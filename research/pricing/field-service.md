## Notes
- **Trials:** Jobber 14 days, no card. Housecall Pro 14 days, no card. GorillaDesk 14 days, no card, free data migration. ServiceTitan's demo page advertises "2 months FREE" for new sign-ups (https://www.servicetitan.com/demo).
- **Free tiers:** ServiceM8 Free (1 user, 30 jobs/mo; card payments earn bonus job credits). Workiz Lite free for 2 members (UNCONFIRMED, from an old blog post). Kickserv Flex is $19 and waived at ≥$2,500/mo in online payments.
- **Payment processing:** Housecall Pro charges 2.59% card-present, 2.99% online, and 3.49% keyed-in/card-on-file/Amex. Instapay costs 1% on top. Your rate is locked for the life of an uninterrupted plan (https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/2046930-housecall-pro-payment-processing-options). Jobber's pricing page shows 2.9% + 30¢ for online invoice payments (US/CA/UK), the rate depends on your plan, and ACH is 1% (US) (https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/115009590727-Manage-your-Jobber-Payments-Settings). ServiceM8 Pay in Canada charges 2.69%+30c on Premium/Premium Plus, 2.85% on Growing, 3.10% on Starter and 3.30% on Free, all + 30c. Those rates are "as of Sept 2024" (https://support.servicem8.com/hc/en-us/articles/10116208886927-ServiceM8-Pay-card-processing-fees-payout-speed). Kickserv Premium charges as low as 2.5% + 30c through Stripe.
- **Processing-linked discounts:** Kickserv "Kickback" takes 5% off the subscription if you process at least $1k (Start), $2k (Run), $3k (Scale) or $5k (Premium) a month (https://help.kickserv.com/article/95-kickserv-kickback).
- **Add-ons:** Jobber extra users $29/mo. HCP MAX extra users $35/mo. HCP industry pages (HVAC/electrical) show different packaged prices, such as MAX $649/mo for 8 users and $75 per extra user, and Profit Rhino price book is a $149/mo add-on. ServiceM8 Office Agent Pro is $49/mo. FieldPulse Engage phone is $125/mo for 5 users.
- **Recent changes:** Jobber now offers 3 billing terms (no commitment / 1-yr / annual) and a 1-user Core at $29 annual. Kickserv renamed Lite/Standard/Business to Start/Run/Scale and added Flex. Workiz's lineup is unclear: vendor pages mention Standard/Pro/Ultimate, Lite, and Pro/Ultimate/Elite. Workiz plans are annual contracts, which can be paid monthly (https://help.workiz.com/hc/en-us/articles/45353069664785-Billing-FAQ). GorillaDesk moved from flat $49/$99 to per-route pricing.
- **CAD:** Only ServiceM8 showed CAD pricing (/ca/). Jobber and Housecall Pro say USD only. No CAD page turned up for the others.

## Price gaps
- **Cheapest paid entry:** Kickserv Flex at $19/mo (UNCONFIRMED). Among verified figures, Jobber Core at $29/mo (annual, 1 user, USD) and ServiceM8 Starter at $29/mo (unlimited users, capped at 50 jobs). Housecall Pro's solo plan costs $59 to $79. Billed month-to-month, the cheapest verified solo plan is $49 (Jobber Core).
- **Mid-tier clusters at about $149–$230/mo for about 5 users:** Jobber Connect $149 or Grow $229 (annual), HCP Essentials $149 annual / $189 monthly, Kickserv Scale $199, Service Fusion Starter ~$208. Going to the top tier roughly doubles the price: HCP MAX $299–$329, Jobber Plus $399, Service Fusion Pro ~$533.
- **Where per-user pricing jumps:** Past the included seats. Jobber charges +$29 per user after 5, HCP +$35 per user after 8, FieldPulse ~$99 per seat (UNCONFIRMED), and ServiceTitan ~$245–$500 per tech. A 10-tech shop is charged per user everywhere except ServiceM8, GorillaDesk and Service Fusion, which have unlimited users but cap jobs or routes instead, or charge more up front.
- **Missing tiers:** None of the leaders lists CAD except ServiceM8. ServiceTitan, FieldPulse and Workiz's upper tiers have no public pricing. HCP and Jobber have no free tier, and the month-to-month solo floor is $49–$79.
- **Undercut opportunity:** A new entrant could offer a CAD-priced solo/small-crew plan around $25–$39 month-to-month with flat 2–5 users (beating Jobber Core's $49 no-commit and HCP Basic's $79 monthly), or transparent flat pricing with unlimited users below ~$149 for 5–10 techs. It could also undercut on card processing, below HCP's 2.59%–3.49% and Jobber's 2.9% + 30¢.
