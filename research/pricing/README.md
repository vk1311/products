# Competitor pricing research

**Date checked:** 2026-11-04 · **Markets:** Canada + US · **Currencies:** recorded exactly as each vendor shows them (no conversion)

## TL;DR

- **Canadian pricing is rare.** In most categories only 1–2 vendors publish CAD prices. Where they do, the CAD figure is often *lower* than the USD figure (QuickBooks, Square, Zoho). Clear CAD pricing is an easy way to stand out.
- **The "solo/small team" rung is missing at the market leaders.** Square goes from $0 to $49. Jobber/Housecall Pro start at $49–$79 month-to-month. QuickBooks goes from $38 to $85. BambooHR charges $250 flat even for 10 people. Cheap challengers (Setmore $5, ServiceM8 $29, Invoice Ninja $14, Deel $5 PEPM) show there is demand there.
- **Per-user pricing jumps after the included seats.** Jobber charges +$29 per user after 5 and Housecall Pro +$35 after 8. QuickBooks steps 1 → 3 → 5 → 25 users. Flat or unlimited-user pricing (ServiceM8, Xero, Vagaro's 7+ cap, GlossGenius Platinum) undercuts the leaders as teams grow.
- **Mid-market pricing is often hidden.** Guest guides (5–50 properties), HR (Rippling, Humi/Employment Hero HR tiers) and field service (ServiceTitan, FieldPulse) are mostly quote-only or set by an on-site calculator.
- **Payment fees are where the money is.** Wave, Square, Fresha, Booksy, Touch Stay and Housecall Pro all keep subscriptions low or free and earn on card fees (2.4–3.5%) or on commissions (5–30% on new clients or upsells).

## ⚠️ How these figures were checked (read before using)

The cloud environment's network policy **blocked direct access to every vendor website** (getjobber.com, squareup.com, quickbooks.intuit.com, bamboohr.com, hostfully.com and others all failed). So **no pricing page was loaded directly**. Every figure comes from **web-search results limited to the vendor's own domain**, meaning the search engine's indexed copy of the vendor's pricing, help or docs page.

Status labels used in the tables:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** / **Verified (snippet)** / **Verified\*** | Figure appeared in a search result from the vendor's own domain for the cited URL, with no conflicting vendor figure. These are reliable but **not** checked on the live page. Re-check before external or decision use. |
| **UNCONFIRMED** | Vendor pages conflict, the source is stale, or it is third-party (URL given). Treat as indicative only. |
| **Not found / Quote-only** | No public price, or no figure surfaced. |

### Cross-category takeaways

1. **CAD-first, transparent pricing.** Nobody owns it. Guest guides have zero CAD pricing. Field service and appointments have one vendor each with CAD (ServiceM8; Square/Vagaro).
2. **A real $15–$39 "solo / small crew" tier.** The leaders all skip it: Square $0→$49, Jobber/HCP $49–$79 month-to-month, QBO $38, BambooHR $250 flat.
3. **Flat pricing for up to ~10 seats.** Per-seat charges past 3–8 users are where leader pricing climbs fastest.
4. **Lower or simpler payment fees.** Leaders earn heavily on 2.4–3.5% card fees, $0.30–$0.60 fixed fees and 5–30% commissions. A lower or flatter rate is an easy thing to compare against.
