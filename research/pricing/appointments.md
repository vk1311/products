A row is "Verified" when the vendor-domain snippet shows the figure clearly and no other vendor page contradicts it. It is "UNCONFIRMED" when vendor pages disagree or the snippet doesn't make clear which plan a figure belongs to. Re-check the live pages once the domains are allowed.

## Notes
- **Free tiers:** Square Free (1 location), Setmore Free (up to 4 users), SimplyBook.me Free (50 bookings, 1 provider) and Calendly Free. Acuity, Vagaro, Fresha, Booksy and GlossGenius have no free subscription tier in these sources.
- **Trials:** Square Plus and Premium have a 30-day free trial. Fresha has a 7-day trial, which its FAQ says is for teams. Acuity has a 7-day trial.
- **Processing is the hidden price lever.** Square's in-person rate falls by plan: 2.6% + 15¢ on Free, 2.5% on Plus, 2.4% on Premium (US). Fresha charges 2.29% + $0.20 in person with a low subscription. Vagaro steps down to 2.29% + $0.19 above $4K/mo volume. Booksy's card reader is 2.49% + $0.10. Vagaro and Square both charge about 3.5% for keyed or card-not-present payments.
- **Marketplace / new-client fees:** Fresha takes 20% of the first marketplace booking ($6 minimum; cap not found). Booksy Boost takes 30% of the first visit (max $100) and has no monthly fee. Square, Acuity, Setmore and GlossGenius charge no new-client commission in these sources.
- **Add-ons:** Fresha Team Connect costs $2.95 per member/mo. SimplyBook.me bills SMS, WhatsApp, AI-booking and extra bookings on usage. Calendly sells Standard Plus and Teams Plus (Notetaker, Callie), but the price was not found. Vagaro's $23.99 US base is labelled "limited time".
- **Currency:** Only Square (pricing page plus structured data) and Vagaro (support article) show CAD. SimplyBook.me prices in EUR. The Fresha snippets show "$" with no currency label.
- **Legacy pricing in circulation:** A 2022 Square Community post shows $40/$90 per location. The Square CA help article shows $49/$149 per location, which doesn't match the CA pricing page's $35/$85 CAD. Acuity's two Premium figures probably reflect a recent price change.

## Price gaps
- **Solo pros with a team of 1:** The cheapest paid entry points are Setmore Pro ($5 per user/mo annual), SimplyBook.me Basic (€11.90/mo annual) and Acuity Starter ($16/mo annual). Square's step from Free straight to $49/mo Plus (US) leaves no paid tier between $0 and $49.
- **Small teams of about 5 staff (US list, monthly):** Vagaro is about $63.99 ($23.99 + 4 × $10). Fresha Team is about $74.75 (5 × $14.95). Booksy is about $109.99 ($29.99 + 4 × $20). GlossGenius Gold is $56 flat, and Square Plus is $49 flat per location. Square and GlossGenius are flat-priced, which is cheaper than per-staff pricing as teams grow.
- **Large or multi-staff teams:** Square Premium is $149 per location (US). GlossGenius Platinum is $168/mo with unlimited staff. Vagaro caps at $83.99/mo (US) for 7+ calendars. Vagaro's cap undercuts both by $65–$84/mo.
- **US to Canada:** Square CA Plus/Premium list at $35/$85 CAD against $49/$149 USD. Vagaro CA base is $35 CAD against $23.99 USD. Square's Canadian list price is nominally well below its US price.
- **Processing versus subscription:** Fresha and Booksy pair low subscriptions with marketplace commissions of 20–30% on new clients. Square charges no commission but needs the $149 Premium plan to reach its lowest card rate (2.4% + 15¢).
