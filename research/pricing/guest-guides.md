## Notes

- **Free tiers:** Hostfully gives 1 free guidebook forever. Touch Stay's Free plan allows unlimited guidebooks but caps each at 20 guest views per month and takes a 5% store commission. Hospitable Essentials is $0 but has no guidebook.
- **Trials:** Touch Stay offers a 14-day trial with no card. Guidey lists a free trial (third-party source). Hostfully and Chekin trials were not confirmed in this session.
- **Commission and upsell models:** Touch Stay's store commission falls by tier: 5% Free, 3% Starter, 2.5% Growth, 2% Pro. Enso takes 5% of upsells, capped at $25 per transaction. Chekin's upsell module has no monthly fee and takes 10% of guest spend.
- **Minimums and bundles:** Duve uses monthly minimums ($120–$200). Chekin requires at least 3 units. Hostfully's larger plans are sold as guidebook bundles (65 for $325/mo, 180 for $9,720/yr), and billing follows the count of published guidebooks.
- **Fees:** YourWelcome charges no setup fee, but its FAQ lists a per-purchase fee (£10 UK / €15 EU / $15 rest of world). Its FAQ mentions 12- and 24-month auto-renewing terms, while the pricing page shows a 1-year contract.
- **Annual discount:** Hostfully's 180-guidebook annual bundle is marked "10% OFF". Chekin shows its lowest per-property prices on annual billing only.

## Price gaps

- **1-property host vs 20-property manager:** A single host pays $0 on Hostfully Free or Touch Stay Free. For 20 properties, Touch Stay's starting rate gives $8.25 × 20 = $165/mo, while Chekin Premium gives €5.95 × 20 = €119/mo. Hostfully's Pro price for 2–64 guidebooks is not published. Only the 65-guidebook bundle ($325/mo, $5.00 per guidebook) is visible.
- **No CAD pricing anywhere:** Every vendor shows USD, GBP or EUR. Touch Stay's prices appear to change by visitor location, so a page could show a different currency to Canadian buyers.
- **Mid-portfolio opacity:** Touch Stay's tier prices are set by a calculator (only "from $8.25" is indexed). Hostfully Pro, Enso and Duve Enterprise are quote-only. So the 5–50 property range, where most growth managers sit, has no checkable list price.
- **Hardware premium:** YourWelcome costs $449.99 per property per year (about $37.50/mo), roughly 4.5 times Touch Stay's starting rate. It bundles a tablet, which pure digital tools do not.
- **Bundled vs standalone:** Chekin and Duve sell guidebooks as part of check-in or guest-app suites (Chekin's guidebook is a premium add-on, so Basic €3.95 does not include it). Hospitable has no native guidebook at all.
- **Small operators priced out of Duve:** Duve's $120/mo minimum works out to $120 per property for a 1-unit host, against $0–$12.50 for the guidebook-only tools.
