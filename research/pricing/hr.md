# HR / HRIS & payroll (BambooHR / Humi / Rippling competitors)

**Scope:** Published list pricing for small-business HR/HRIS tools, with payroll where it is bundled or sold alongside, in Canada and the US. BambooHR, Humi and Rippling are included as benchmarks. Prices are shown exactly as the vendor shows them, with no currency conversion.

**Date checked: 2026-11-04**

**Method caveat (read first):** WebFetch could not reach any vendor host in this session. bamboohr.com, humi.ca, rippling.com and gusto.com all failed with DNS errors from the session's network allowlist, so no pricing page was read directly. Every figure below comes from WebSearch results limited to the vendor's own domain. Those results are snippets and summaries of the vendor page, not a direct read of it.
- **Verified\***: a single, consistent figure was shown on the vendor's own pricing, help or legal page in a vendor-domain search snippet. Re-check it on the live page before publishing externally.
- **UNCONFIRMED**: vendor-domain sources conflict, the snippet is stale, or the figure is third-party.
- **Quote-only**: the vendor's pricing page routes to sales.
- **Not found**: no figure was surfaced.

## Price gaps

- **Transparent CA HR + payroll bundle:** In Canada, payroll-only is CA$60–100 for 10 employees, but published HR + payroll tops out at Knit Complete (CA$130). Humi/EH and BambooHR either hide HR pricing ("Custom") or price HR separately. A clearly priced CA bundle around CA$10–13 all-in PEPM is open ground.
- **Micro-company penalty:** BambooHR's ≤25 flat rate works out to $25/employee at 10 employees ($250), against $3–5 PEPM for Deel or Zoho HR-only. Minimums (EH Managed Payroll $500, EH Payroll $70) also hit companies with fewer than 10 employees. Pure PEPM pricing with no base fee is under-served for HR plus payroll.
- **US: DIY payroll to PEO jump:** Gusto runs $109–400 for 10 employees, then Justworks PEO jumps to $790+. Nothing published sits in the ~$400–800 "HR + payroll + benefits admin, non-PEO" band.
- **Cross-border CA + US under one published price:** Gusto and Justworks are US-only. Wagepoint, Knit and EH CA are Canada-only. Rippling and Deel span both but are quote-only (Rippling) or HR-only (Deel HR Core). No vendor publishes a single CA + US payroll + HR price.
- **Module-based pricing makes totals hard to predict:** Rippling, Factorial and Zoho publish only "from" prices, so a buyer can't work out a full 10-employee cost without a quote.
