# Invoicing & accounting (Wave / QuickBooks competitors)

**Scope:** Small-business invoicing and accounting, Canada + US. Benchmarks: Wave, QuickBooks Online (QBO). Competitors: Xero, FreshBooks, Zoho Books, Zoho Invoice, Sage Accounting (Canada), Invoice Ninja. QBO's new Canadian **Lite** plan and US **QuickBooks Free** tier were added because they sit directly in the Wave tier.

**Date checked: 2026-11-04**

**How figures were collected (read this first):** In this session, WebFetch could not reach any vendor host (waveapps.com, quickbooks.intuit.com and xero.com all returned DNS/network-policy failures), and shell curl is blocked. Every figure below therefore comes from **WebSearch results limited to the vendor's own domain**. Those results are snippets the search tool summarised from the vendor page. They are not a direct read of the live page, which may be JS-rendered or geo-dependent.
- **Verified (snippet)** = seen in a vendor-domain search result for that page, with no conflicting vendor figure.
- **UNCONFIRMED** = vendor-domain sources conflict, the page is flagged as old, or the only source is a vendor community forum. The reason is given in the row.
- **Not found** = no vendor-domain figure surfaced.
- Prices are regular (list) prices. Promos are listed under Notes. Currency is recorded as shown, with no conversion. Taxes are extra unless stated.

## Price gaps

- **Free to first paid tier:** The free tools step up to US$14–25/mo. Invoice Ninja's free plan is capped at 5 clients, then Pro is $14. Wave is free, then Pro is US$19 / CA$25. QuickBooks Free (2 invoices/mo) steps to Solopreneur at $20. Zoho Books Free (under $50K revenue) steps to Standard at $20 / $15 annual. FreshBooks has no free tier; Lite starts at US$23 / CA$26 and is capped at 5 clients.
- **CAD is not priced at a premium:** QBO's Canadian list prices are numerically lower than the US ones: C$30 / C$70 / C$110 / C$220 against US$38 / $85 / $140 / $340. Zoho Books Professional is C$35 against US$50, and FreshBooks Plus is C$42 against US$43. Wave (CA$25 vs US$19) and FreshBooks Lite (C$26 vs US$23) are the exceptions, with CAD figures above USD.
- **QBO entry-to-mid cliff:** US Simple Start $38 to Essentials $85 is +124%, and 1 user to 3 users. In Canada, EasyStart C$30 to Essentials C$70 is +133%. Plus to Advanced roughly doubles in both countries (US$140 to $340; C$110 to C$220).
- **Usage caps on starter plans:** Xero Early/Starter caps 20 invoices and 5 bills per month. The next tier is 2.2× (US) or 2.4× (CA) the price: US$27 to $59, C$25 to C$60. FreshBooks Lite to Plus (5 to 50 clients) is US$23 to $43.
- **User-count cliff:** Sage Start is 1 user at C$23. Standard has unlimited users at C$50 (2.2×). Xero includes unlimited users on every plan, while QBO steps 1 / 3 / 5 / 25 users.
- **Payments take-rate:** Wave's fixed fee is $0.60 per card transaction, against $0.30 at FreshBooks. Bank/ACH is about 1% at Wave, FreshBooks and QuickBooks. For small invoices, this is where Wave's free plan makes its money.
