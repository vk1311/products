## Why this pack is different

**Made for business owners, not engineers.** Every workflow has a single **Config** box at the start. Type your business name, email and links there, connect your accounts from a dropdown, and click Activate.

**Thought through, not thrown together.** Reminders never go out twice. Spam bots are filtered. Old blog posts won't flood your feeds. The uptime monitor alerts you once, not every 5 minutes. ## FAQ

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You fill in a settings box and pick your accounts from dropdowns. The code that does the clever bits (like working out which invoices are overdue) is already written, and the parts you might want to change (wording, timings) are labeled in plain English.

**Will this work on n8n Cloud?**
Yes. Every workflow uses only n8n's built-in nodes. There are no community nodes or custom installs.

**Are any API keys or accounts included?**
No. The files contain placeholders only. You connect your own accounts securely inside n8n, so your data stays yours.

**I don't use Slack / Telegram / Google Sheets. Can I swap them?**
Yes. n8n has hundreds of built-in integrations. Swap a Slack node for Microsoft Teams, Discord or email in a couple of clicks. The READMEs point out the common swaps.

**How long does setup take?**
Most workflows take 10–20 minutes the first time, and faster after that because your logins are reused.

**Can I use these for my clients?**
Yes. Setting them up for clients is included in the license. You just can't resell the pack itself.

**What if I get stuck?**
Every workflow has its own README with setup, testing and troubleshooting tips, and the main guide covers the common snags (Gmail app passwords, sheet IDs, timezones, test vs live URLs).
