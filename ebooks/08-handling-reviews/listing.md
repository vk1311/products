# Gumroad listing — Handling Online Reviews

## Title
Handling Online Reviews

## Subtitle
Ask for them, reply to them, and deal with the hard ones — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
A practical guide for Canadian sole proprietors and small teams who want a calm, fair way to handle online reviews. It walks you through asking every customer in the same way, replying to positive, mixed and negative reviews, and dealing with fake, misdirected or threatening ones without making things worse.

You will learn the rules that matter, in plain words: what the Competition Bureau has said about fake reviews and incentives, Google's rules on incentives and review gating, and how to keep customers' personal information out of your replies.

Inside: chapters on asking, replying, reporting and building a weekly routine; copy-ready ask messages for in person, text, email and receipts; ten reply templates; step-by-step escalation guidance; a feedback log; a team roles worksheet; and a one-page checklist.

Format: PDF, about 23 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets.

This guide is general information, not legal advice.
