# Gumroad listing — Pricing Your Services

## Title
Pricing Your Services

## Subtitle
A practical method for Canadian service businesses — work out what your time really costs and set prices you can explain

## Description
For Canadian cleaners, bookkeepers, landscapers, photographers, trades, consultants and other service owners who are unsure whether their prices cover their costs. Instead of guessing or copying a competitor, this guide walks you through a clear method: set an owner pay target, list your overhead, count your billable hours and find your true hourly cost. From there it covers hourly, flat and package pricing, value-based thinking, minimum charges, travel and rush fees, materials markup, and how GST/HST fits into the prices you show. Later chapters cover honest market research, rate cards, discounts, raising prices and answering price objections, with four worked examples. Inside: a true hourly cost worksheet, overhead list, job pricing worksheet, package builder, rate card layout, price increase letter and email, objection responses, an annual review checklist and an action checklist. PDF, 23 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets. General information only, not tax, legal or accounting advice.
