## Title
Invoicing Made Simple

## Subtitle
Clear invoices, fair terms, and calm follow-up when payment is late — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Invoicing Made Simple is for Canadian sole proprietors and small teams who do the work first and get paid after: trades, cleaners, salons, consultants, photographers, tutors, and landscapers.

It walks you through what belongs on an invoice, including GST/HST details if you are registered, how to number and date your invoices, and how to set clear payment terms before the job starts. You will find plain-language chapters on deposits and progress billing, common Canadian payment methods and their trade-offs, sending invoices promptly, choosing between software, spreadsheets and templates, keeping records, and handling late payments, disputes and credit notes calmly.

Inside are copy-ready templates: a sample invoice, payment terms wording, four reminder emails from friendly heads-up to final notice, a credit note, a decisions worksheet, and a one-page checklist.

Format: PDF, 23 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets.

This guide is general information, not legal, tax, or accounting advice.
