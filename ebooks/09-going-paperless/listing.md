# Gumroad listing — Going Paperless

## Title
Going Paperless

## Subtitle
A practical plan to cut the clutter and keep your records safe — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Tired of shoeboxes of receipts, a glove box full of paper, and a filing cabinet nobody wants to open? This guide walks Canadian small-business owners through a realistic move to less paper, one step at a time.

You will learn what the Canada Revenue Agency expects from your records, how to scan receipts so they stay readable, and why to check the rules before you shred anything. It covers a simple folder structure and file-naming system, digital invoices, payments, e-signatures and intake forms, choosing cloud storage, your privacy duties, password and device security, and backups you actually test.

Inside: six practical chapters, a flexible four-week rollout schedule, a monthly routine, and printable templates including a paper audit worksheet, naming rules card, scan-day checklist, security checklist, backup log and retention notes sheet.

Format: PDF, 24 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets.

General information only, not legal, tax or accounting advice.
