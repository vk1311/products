## Title
GST/HST Basics

## Subtitle
Register, charge, claim and file sales tax without the guesswork — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Not sure whether you should be charging GST or HST, or what to do once you start? This plain-language guide is written for Canadian sole proprietors and small teams: trades, cleaners, salons, consultants, tutors, food trucks and small shops. Examples use Nova Scotia businesses, but the guide works anywhere in Canada and points out where rules change by province.

Inside, you will find clear chapters on GST and HST rates, place of supply, the small supplier rule with worked examples, voluntary registration, showing tax on invoices, tax-included pricing, zero-rated and exempt sales, input tax credits, filing and paying, setting money aside, record keeping, and what to do if you are late or made an error.

Includes a registration decision worksheet, quarterly revenue tracker, HST set-aside worksheet, client notice template, filing-day checklist, invoice tax-line examples and a one-page action checklist.

PDF, about 24 pages, Letter size, printable worksheets.

General information only, not tax, legal or accounting advice.
