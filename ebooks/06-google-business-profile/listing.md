## Title
Your Google Business Profile

## Subtitle
Set it up properly, keep it accurate, and stay within the rules — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Your Google Business Profile can be one of the first things a new customer sees, and Google's guidelines and screens keep changing. This plain-language guide is for Canadian sole proprietors and small teams who want a profile that is accurate, complete and within the rules.

Inside, you will learn how to claim or create your profile without duplicates, choose between showing your address and listing a service area, use your real-world name and the right categories, get through verification, write a clear description, set holiday hours that reflect your province, add real photos, ask for reviews fairly, and protect your profile from unwanted changes and suspension.

Includes a profile information worksheet, three sample descriptions, a photo shot list, a year of post ideas, a review request message, and a 20-minute monthly maintenance checklist.

PDF, about 23 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets. Independent of Google. General information only, not legal or marketing advice.
