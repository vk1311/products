# Gumroad listing — Hiring Your First Employee

## Title
Hiring Your First Employee

## Subtitle
From job posting to first pay day — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Thinking about hiring your first employee but not sure where to start? This plain-language guide walks Canadian small-business owners through the process, from deciding whether you can afford help to running your first payroll. Written for trades, cleaners, salons, consultants, shops, and other small teams, it explains how to tell an employee from a contractor, write a fair job posting, interview and check references, and make a written offer. It shows how to open a CRA payroll account, collect SIN and TD1 forms, handle CPP, EI, and income tax deductions, remit on schedule, and issue T4 slips and Records of Employment. It also covers provincial employment standards, workers' compensation, health and safety, employee privacy, and the first 90 days, with Nova Scotia as the main example. Inside: a hiring-readiness worksheet, job posting outline, interview questions, offer letter outline, new-hire checklist, and first-week plan. PDF, 24 pages, Letter size, printable. General information, not legal, tax, or payroll advice.
