# Gumroad listing — Quotes That Get a Clear Yes

## Title
Quotes That Get a Clear Yes

## Subtitle
Write quotes and estimates customers understand, accept and can hold you to — a plain-language guide for Canadian trades and service businesses

## Description
For Canadian trades and service businesses that price work job by job: painters, plumbers, renovators, landscapers, cleaners, caterers, consultants and more. If your quotes lead to "I thought that was included" conversations, or you are never sure what to put in writing, this guide walks you through the whole process in plain language.

Inside: the difference between a quote and an estimate, and why provincial consumer protection rules matter; questions to ask before you price; writing inclusions, exclusions, assumptions and allowances; a worked pricing example with labour, materials, markup, overhead and contingency; showing GST/HST; deposits, validity dates and payment schedules; change orders; written and electronic acceptance; and handling "that's too expensive."

Includes a quote-prep worksheet, quote layout, scope wording, acceptance block, change-order form, follow-up emails, quote log and a one-page checklist. PDF, 24 pages, Letter size, printable.

General information only, not legal or tax advice.
