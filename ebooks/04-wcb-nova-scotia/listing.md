## Title
WCB Basics for Nova Scotia Employers

## Subtitle
Registration, assessments, injuries, and return to work — a plain-language guide for small businesses in Nova Scotia

## Description
Hiring your first few workers in Nova Scotia and not sure where WCB fits in? This guide explains workers' compensation in plain language for owners of small businesses such as cleaning companies, trades, cafés, salons, and landscaping crews.

Inside you will find chapters on what WCB Nova Scotia does, how it differs from workplace safety rules, how to work out whether you need to register, coverage for owners, how assessments work in concept, reporting payroll, checking contractors, what to do when a worker is hurt, return to work, and simple injury prevention.

Templates include a "do I need to register" worksheet, a payroll-estimate worksheet, an injury-response checklist, an incident notes form, a contractor check, a return-to-work outline, and a yearly task calendar.

Format: PDF, about 23 pages, Letter size, with printable worksheets. This guide is independent and not affiliated with WCB Nova Scotia. It is general information, not legal advice.
