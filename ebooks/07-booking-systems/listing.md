# Gumroad listing — Online Booking Made Simple

## Title
Online Booking Made Simple

## Subtitle
Choose and set up an appointment system that fits your business — a plain-language guide for Canadian small businesses

## Description
Thinking about letting customers book appointments, classes or service calls online, but not sure where to start? This guide is for Canadian sole proprietors and small teams who take bookings: salons, clinics, groomers, tutors, cleaners and trades. It helps you decide whether you need a booking system at all, understand the main types of tools, and work out your must-haves before you spend money. You will learn what privacy questions to ask vendors, how to test a tool during a free trial, and how to set up services, buffers, availability and staff access step by step. It also covers deposit and cancellation policies, reminder messages and anti-spam basics, launching to your customers, and switching tools later. Inside: a needs worksheet, a vendor comparison table, vendor privacy questions, sample policy wording, message templates, a phone script and a launch checklist. PDF, about 24 pages, Letter size, printable worksheets. General information only, not legal, tax or privacy advice.
